THE ATHLETE

A journey built

Mokitha Piyarathna is a former amateur boxer based in Wales, United Kingdom. His amateur boxing career is reported as spanning 2024 to 2026, with Maerdy Boxing Club listed as his former club.

Across amateur tournaments, Mokitha reports earning 13 gold medals, one WWB gold medal, one silver medal and one bronze medal. This profile brings together the career details and photographs supplied by the athlete.

This is a personal athlete profile, not an official governing-body record. Bout-by-bout results, dates, opponents and win/loss totals have not been supplied.
